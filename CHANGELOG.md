# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Roll from the action bar.** Every option with a roll shows its modifier or dice and rolls when you click it: on the tile, in a *Roll* row in the detail, and in the text. That includes the sheet's attacks and damage, spells and items, and the rules options with this unit's numbers: Hide (Stealth), Influence, Search and Study (every skill, best on the tile), Escape a Grapple, Stabilize, Unarmed Strike, Improvised Weapon (now with its to-hit and 1d4 + Strength damage), 5E Grapple and Shove, Climb and Swim (Athletics), Dismount (Dex save), and the DMG's Disarm (your best weapon attack), Overrun, Shove Aside (with Disadvantage), Tumble and Climb onto a Bigger Creature. Shift rolls with advantage, Ctrl/⌘ with disadvantage, the public / private switch is shared with the card, 5.5E exhaustion is applied, and a natural 20 makes that option's damage roll a critical hit. The result shows in the detail and goes to the roll log and notifications as usual.
- **Vision HUD, pick whose vision:** *Choose units…* lists every party member and creature in the scene to tick one by one (or *All* / *None* per group), alongside the selected tokens, the whole party or everyone. Right-click tokens → **Show / hide vision aura** does the same from the map. The GM's see-through darkness fog shows what the picked units see.
- **Players tab (GM):** a new tab next to the Tracker with a card for each party member: HP with a bar and temp HP, AC, initiative, passive Perception, Insight and Investigation, spell save DC (or Speed for non-casters), saves (proficient ones highlighted), speeds, senses, resistances and immunities, conditions and who plays them. The top line has the party's lowest passives, for secret checks. It follows the scene live; click a name for the full card.
- **The action bar follows the unit's conditions.** *Escape a Grapple* only shows while the unit is Grappled, *Stand Up* only while it's Prone (and *Drop Prone* only while it isn't). *Mount or Dismount* is now two options: **Mount**, or **Dismount** once the unit has the new **Mounted** status (in *Change status* and on the card, in both editions).
- **Action bar: one list, with Attack gathering every way to attack.** The separate ⚔ Combat tab is gone: the sheet's entries and what anyone can do by the rules sit together, sorted by cost (Actions, Bonus Actions, Reactions, Legendary, Spells, Limited features, Movement, Free & other); the cost filters cover both. Hover (or click) **Attack** to reveal every option for the Attack action: the sheet's weapons and a creature's Multiattack and natural weapons first, then Unarmed Strike, Grapple, Shove, Improvised Weapon, Disarm and Shove Aside. Extra Attack shows on the tile ("2 attacks"). Two-Weapon Fighting sits with the Bonus Actions and Opportunity Attack with the Reactions.
- **5.5E weapon swapping:** in a 5.5E room, each attack of the Attack action can equip or unequip one weapon (draw, pick up, sheathe, stow or drop it), before or after that attack. This shows as *Equip or Unequip a Weapon* under Attack, and *Interact with an Object* no longer lists drawing or sheathing a weapon. An attack replaced by something else (the Eldritch Knight's War Magic cantrip, a Grapple, a Shove) still gets its swap. *Equip or Unequip a Weapon* notes that a spellcasting focus isn't a weapon unless it's a staff that also counts as a Quarterstaff, so drawing or stowing one uses the free object interaction (now in its text). *Magic* explains free hands for Somatic and Material components, and the holy-symbol-on-a-shield and War Caster ways around them. 5E (2014) rooms keep the old wording.
- **Magic and Help reveal their options too, and Multiattack comes first.** Hover (or click) **Magic** (*Cast a Spell* in 5E) for the unit's spells cast with an action, a creature's spell lists and any action that says it's a Magic action. Other spells sit with their casting time: bonus-action spells with the Bonus Actions, reaction spells with the Reactions, rituals and longer casts in their own group. The ✦ Spells filter still lists them all. In 5.5E, **Help** reveals its ordinary use (helping with a check or an attack) next to **Stabilize** (first aid is done with the Help action); in 5E it stays its own Action. A creature's **Multiattack** is the first Action, ahead of Attack.
- **The action bar reads the inventory, and leaves out what a unit can't do.** Items you use in combat sit under the action that uses them. Potions go under *Drink or Administer a Potion*, and wands, staffs, rods, scrolls and other magic items you activate go under *Magic*; in 5E they go under *Use an Object*. Caltrops, ball bearings, a healer's kit, manacles, oil and rope go under *Utilize*. In 5.5E, thrown Acid, Alchemist's Fire and Holy Water go in the Attack row. Magic items activated with a Bonus Action or a Reaction sit with those. Armor, passive rings and weapons don't appear (weapon attacks are already there). *Magic* only shows if the unit has spells, a magic item or a Magic action, so a wolf has none. *Cast a Spell* (5E) needs spells, the potion action needs a potion, and *Two-Weapon Fighting* needs two Light weapons (or Dual Wielder); hover it to see them. Re-sync party members from D&D Beyond once so their items carry their type, magic and weapon properties (older syncs are read from item names).
- **DMG optional options are regular options now,** listed with the rest (still tagged DMG). The GM can leave them out with a new room setting (Combat tab → Room settings), on by default.
- **Homebrew actions:** the GM clicks **＋ Homebrew** on any action bar to add an action for the whole room: a name, what it costs (Action, one attack of the Attack action, Bonus Action, Reaction, free or movement), a short line for the tile and how it works. It shows on every unit's bar in the right group (attack ones under Attack); click it to edit or remove it.
- **Manage initiative (right-click, GM):** *Add to / Remove from initiative* and *Roll initiative* are one screen now. Roll for everyone selected, or only those without an initiative; add them to the fight or take them out; or type a value (typing one for a unit outside the fight brings it in). Each row shows the unit's modifier, how its initiative was rolled, and its place in the order.
- **Save to collection is on the unit card** (GM, under ⚔ Actions) instead of the right-click menu.
- **Change status (right-click, GM):** *Damage / heal*, *Conditions* and *Mark as dead* are one screen now. Damage (half on a save), heal, temp HP or set HP, and turn conditions on and off with a click: **Dead**, **Stable** and **Death Saves** (which exclude each other) next to every other condition, Concentration, Exhaustion with its level and any custom effects already on the units. On several units at once, a dashed chip means only some of them have it. Durations, notes and custom effects are under *With a duration, a note, or a custom effect…*.
- **Light & vision for dim and dark scenes:** set a scene to **Dim** or **Dark** in **Tracker → Light & vision**, then click **👁 Show Vision HUD** (each person in their own browser; nothing is drawn while it's off) to see what every creature can see. Carried lights draw yellow circles (bright and dim), and senses draw labelled outlines: darkvision, Devil's Sight, truesight, blindsight and tremorsense, for the selected tokens, the party or all units. The legend explains what's clear, lightly obscured or heavily obscured. Light a torch, lantern, *Light* cantrip or any other light from the unit card (lights on the sheet come first); players light their own. The card also says in words what the unit sees in the scene's light. Players only see senses for their own units and party members.
- **Darkness fog (Vision HUD):** with the HUD on in a dark (or dim) scene, the map is covered with black where your units can't see, and a haze where it's only lightly obscured. Only your units' senses and the lights around them cut through it. The GM's fog is see-through. To keep creatures in the dark hidden whatever players choose, the GM can force the fog on players for a scene (off by default).
- **Action bar (BG3-style), for reference:** right-click a unit → *Actions*, or **⚔ Actions** on the card or in the player panel, opens a hotbar at the bottom of the screen. It shows everything the unit can do, sorted by **● Action / ▲ Bonus Action / ◆ Reaction / ★ Legendary**, plus spells and limited features. A **⚔ Combat** tab has every combat option from the Player's Handbook (Dash, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Unarmed Strike, Grapple, Shove, Two-Weapon Fighting, Opportunity Attack, jumping, climbing, standing up…) and the Dungeon Master's Guide optional ones (Disarm, Mark, Overrun, Shove Aside, Tumble, Climb onto a Bigger Creature), with each character's numbers (to-hit, Grapple / Shove DC, skill bonuses, jump distances). Click a tile for a plain explanation of how it works: the cost, what to roll and against what, damage, saves, spell level, Concentration, limits and recharge. It's read-only (nothing is counted) and can open on your turn. Party members' lists come from their D&D Beyond sync, which now includes each spell's casting time (re-sync once to get it).
- **Wild Shape for druids (5.5E rules):** right-click a druid's token → *Wild Shape*, or use the button on the unit card. Keep your **Known Forms** (4 / 6 / 8 by level), picked from the 5etools bestiary: Beasts up to your max CR (Circle of the Moon: druid level ÷ 3), no flying speed before level 8. **Transform** changes the token's picture and size and the card's stat block. You keep your own HP, creature type, mental scores and proficiencies, and gain temp HP (druid level, or 3 × for the Moon). Moon druids also get AC at least 13 + Wis, Circle Spells in beast form, and Wis added to Con saves from level 6. You revert at 0 HP, when Incapacitated, when marked dead, or with **Revert**. Uses are counted: one back on a Short Rest, all on a Long Rest. Druids synced from D&D Beyond are set up automatically. Rooms set to 5E (2014) use the 2014 version (beast HP with damage carrying over).

## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-08

Encounter Butler now runs a whole D&D combat on its own. If you're upgrading from 1.x and the right-click *View unit card* or turn notices don't appear, remove the extension in Owlbear (**Profile → Extensions**) and add the link again.

- **No more Forge!** Encounter Butler stores each unit's stat block and live state (HP, initiative, party) on the token itself and works on its own. Units created with Forge aren't converted: add them again. See [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md) for how each feature was checked.
- **Unit cards:** right-click a unit → *View unit card*, or click a name in the Combat tab. The card shows the whole stat block: portrait, AC, HP, initiative, speed, abilities, saves, skills, senses, defences and every trait, action, reaction, legendary action, spell and item.
- **Clickable dice:** every bonus and damage value on the card rolls. Shift-click for advantage, Ctrl/Cmd-click for disadvantage (or the toggle on the card). After an attack, the card offers its damage, and crit damage (dice doubled) after a natural 20. Rolls are **public** (everyone gets a notification) or **private** (only you; a player's private roll also goes to the GM). Each browser keeps a roll log for the session.
- **Players:** the Encounter Butler button shows players whose turn it is, the initiative order (names or the GM's aliases, pictures and conditions; hidden tokens never appear) and their own units. On their own units they can change HP and initiative, add or remove conditions from the card, roll death saves and Concentration saves, end their turn and use the movement tracker. A room setting (Combat tab) turns player edits off.
- **Party view** at the top of *Add Party*: the party members, which player controls each, their level and HP; add or remove members.
- **Players re-sync their own character from D&D Beyond:** *⟳ D&D Beyond* next to their unit opens their character; the same bookmark sends the sheet back, and the GM's Encounter Butler applies it to that unit only.
- **Initiative tracker** (new first tab): the turn order with Start combat, Next / Previous turn, the round counter, End combat and Reset round & initiative. Type players' initiative rolls, roll everyone or just the creatures, reorder ties, damage or heal from each row (`-7`, `+5`, `12`, `t5` for temp HP), and add units that aren't in the fight yet.
- **Turns:** the player who controls a unit gets "It's your turn" and "You're up next", even with the panel closed. The active unit has a ring on the map, and anyone can have their view follow it. Players can end their own turn.
- **HP, 0 HP and death:** temp HP absorbs damage first and doesn't stack. A party member at 0 HP makes death saves (rolled from their card; damage at 0 HP counts as a failure, and three successes make them Stable); a creature at 0 HP can be marked dead, or automatically by room setting. **Mark as dead** takes a unit out of initiative and passes the turn on if it was theirs; **Revive** undoes it.
- **Conditions:** add any condition (or a custom effect with a note) from the card or, for several units at once, from the right-click *Conditions* action. Exhaustion has levels (in 5.5E, −2 per level on d20 rolls from the card and −5 ft speed per level). A duration (rounds, until the start of a unit's turn, until the end of its next turn) ends on its own with a notice. Conditions show on the card, in the tracker and as a small badge under the token (players see badges for tokens they can see).
- **Concentration:** damage to a concentrating unit puts a Constitution save on its card with the right DC (the higher of 10 and half the damage; capped at 30 in 5.5E); a failed save ends Concentration, and so does dropping to 0 HP.
- **Movement tracker:** during its turn, the active unit's token shows how far it has moved against its speed ("25/30 ft"), red once it's over. *Dash* doubles the limit and *Rewind* moves the token back to the last 5 ft point of its path. The GM and the unit's player both get these controls.
- **Right-click actions (GM)** on one or more selected units: add to / remove from initiative, roll initiative, damage / heal (one amount for all, with half damage for a successful save), mark as dead, add to / remove from party.
- **Names, aliases and portraits:** rename a unit (the token label follows), show it to players under an alias ("Hooded figure", also on the token's label) until revealed, and give it a portrait different from its token art (link or Owlbear library).
- **Your collection:** keep homebrew and favourite creatures in this browser: *Save to collection* in the homebrew form, on a unit's card (*Edit unit*) or on its right-click menu. Search them, tag them, edit them (name, tags, AC, HP, CR, or the whole stat block), start a new one, and put them on the list to add like any creature. *Export file* / *Import file* back the collection up or move it to another computer. Tokens already in a scene keep their own copy.
- **Creature search filters:** narrow the 5etools search by Challenge Rating (range), creature type, environment, size and spellcaster, or set filters with no name to browse every match by CR. Creatures 5etools gives no environment get one from Encounter Butler (marked ≈ in the results).
- **Game system setting:** D&D 5.5E (2024 rules, default) or 5E (2014 rules), in the Combat tab. In 5E the difficulty calculator uses the 2014 thresholds (Easy / Medium / Hard / Deadly) and group multipliers, creatures roll initiative with their Dexterity modifier, cards use the 2014 attack wording, Exhaustion follows the 2014 effects table and there's no Bloodied. Creature search puts the chosen edition's books first and only offers that edition's Player's Handbook, Monster Manual and Dungeon Master's Guide (other books stay available in both).
- Mythic actions are now listed with legendary actions. Dice chips accept any die size (e.g. d3).
- Party files from earlier versions need a fresh sync from D&D Beyond.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased, folded into 2.0.0)

- **New layout: three tabs.** *Add Creatures*, *Add Party Members* and *Combat* replace Monsters / Party / Prepared encounter. Prepared encounters are now a collapsed section at the bottom of Add Creatures.
- **Import homebrewed creature:** upload a stat block JSON file, or start from any published 5etools creature and edit its stat block before adding it. Name it yourself and give it any token (Owlbear library upload, https link, the original 5etools token or the placeholder).
- **Add Party Members:** a one-button **Sync from D&D Beyond**. It opens your campaign, and the bookmark there sends the party straight back (no clipboard step) and syncs it as Forge's Party. The campaign is remembered. Re-drag the bookmark once. Published and homebrewed party members (sidekicks, companions) can be added like creatures. Undo removes tokens a sync created and restores replaced portraits.
- **Combat tab:** encounter difficulty from the 2024 DMG XP budgets (Low / Moderate / High), plus an invented **Extreme** tier for boss fights (1.5× High). Exclude non-hostile creatures or absent characters, set levels, and see it update live. **Roll all initiatives** or **Roll just creatures' initiatives**.
- Party sync no longer rolls initiative by default (the Combat tab does); the old choices are still in *Sync options*.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-04

First public release. Install in Owlbear Rodeo with
`https://corrida-repos.github.io/owlbear-rodeo-butler/manifest.json` (see the [README](README.md)).

- **Monsters tab:** search every 5etools creature (112 books, 2024 versions marked), set quantities, and add them at the centre of the view. Each creature gets its own HP (average or rolled), individually rolled initiative (advantage handled), a Forge! stat block with dice chips, and a place in the tracker. Adds can be undone. Custom creatures can come from a pasted 5etools JSON or quick stats, with art from the Owlbear library, an https link, or a placeholder.
- **Party tab:** the "Copy party for Encounter Butler" bookmarklet reads your D&D Beyond campaign in your own browser. **Paste party** computes each character's HP, AC (including party-stash armor), saves, skills, initiative, attacks, actions, features and spells, and writes them to the matching tokens. It can also create missing tokens, use round D&D Beyond portraits (with undo), and roll initiative.
- **Prepared encounter tab:** import a package with a map, grid alignment, positioned tokens and pre-rolled initiative. Duplicate detection, replace and undo are included.
- Requires the Forge! extension on its default D&D 5E system.
